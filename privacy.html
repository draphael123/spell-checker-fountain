Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

🔒 TL;DR: Fountain collects zero data. Everything stays on your device.

Our Commitment

Fountain Spell Checker is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe your typing data is personal and should never leave your device. This privacy policy explains exactly how we handle (or rather, don't handle) your data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect what you type
  • We do not collect your spelling mistakes
  • We do not collect your corrections
  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not collect your email, name, or any personal information
  • We do not use cookies or tracking pixels

Local Storage

All spelling data (misspellings, corrections, statistics) is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage.local API. This data:

  • Never leaves your browser
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Can be exported or deleted by you at any time

Offline Operation

Fountain works 100% offline. The extension does not make any network requests. The spell-checking dictionary is bundled with the extension and runs entirely in your browser.

Permissions

Fountain requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • storage - To save your spelling history locally on your device
  • activeTab - To detect text fields on the current page
  • host_permissions (all URLs) - To work on any website you visit

These permissions are the minimum required for the extension to function. We do not use them to collect or transmit any data.

Open Source

Fountain is open source. You can inspect the entire codebase on GitHub to verify our privacy claims. We welcome security audits and contributions.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your Fountain data at any time by:

  • Opening the Dashboard and clicking "Reset Data"
  • Uninstalling the extension (this removes all stored data)
  • Clearing your browser's extension storage

Contact Form

If you voluntarily submit feedback through our contact form on the website, that message is processed separately from the extension and is not connected to your spelling data in any way.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Any significant changes will be announced on our GitHub repository.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or use the contact form on our website.

✨ Your privacy matters. That's why we built Fountain to work entirely on your device.
